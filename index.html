PROFILE

Design informed
by engineering.

I am a Mechanical Engineer from Greece with a strong interest in automotive and motorsport engineering, particularly mechanical design, vehicle aerodynamics and computational simulation. My background combines CAD development, CFD analysis, prototyping and practical exposure to vehicle systems.

For my final-year thesis, I developed a Formula 1-inspired race car model and studied its aerodynamic performance through airflow behaviour and pressure distribution. With the Hyperion Drone Team, I contributed to component development, aerodynamic configuration and 3D printing within a collaborative engineering environment.

I am interested in opportunities to develop as a mechanical or automotive design engineer and contribute to the design, analysis and development of high-performance engineering systems.
